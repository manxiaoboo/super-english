# 语法翻译训练 2026-07-29

> 外企程序员日常场景 · 即时诊断模式

---

## 1. 主系表 + but 并列从句

🀄 这个方案看起来可行，但我们还需要更多数据来支持它。

✅ This solution looks feasible but we also need more data to support it.

> 语法：主系表结构（looks + 形容词作表语）+ but 并列两个完整从句。

---

## 2. since + 现在完成时

🀄 自从我加入这家公司以来，已经参与了三个主要项目。

✅ Since I joined this company, I have been involved in three major projects.

用户：Since I joined this company, I have invoted three main projects.
问题：invoted 不是英语单词；main projects → major projects；需用 be involved in 表示"参与"

> 语法：since引导时间状语从句 + 现在完成时（have been involved），表达从过去持续到现在的经历。

---

## 3. must + be + 形容词推测

🀄 他今天没来参加站会，肯定是又堵在路上了。

✅ He didn't show up for the stand-up today. He must be stuck in traffic again.

用户：He didn't join the stand meeting today, he must stucked in traffic again.
问题：stand meeting → stand-up；stucked 不存在，must 后跟原形 be stuck；comma splice

> 语法：must 表达肯定推测，后接原形 be；be stuck in traffic 固定搭配。

---

## 4. 定语从句 + 左二右六

🀄 那个今天早上我们讨论过的 bug 比我想象的严重得多。

✅ The bug that we discussed this morning is much more serious than I thought.

用户：The bug that we discussed this morning is much more serous than what I imagined.
问题：serous → serious（拼写）；than what I imagined → than I thought 更自然

> 语法：名词短语"左二右六"——中心词 bug，左 the，右 that 定语从句。

---

## 5. 泛指冠词 + 并列从句

🀄 我们需要一个既能处理高并发、又容易维护的架构。

✅ We need an architecture that can handle high concurrency and is easy to maintain.

用户：We need an architecture that can handle high concurrency and easy to remain.
问题：remain → maintain（词义混淆）；并列结构缺 is

> 语法：an architecture 泛指"一种架构"；并列从句需保持结构完整。

---

## 6. 最高级 + 现在完成时 ever

🀄 这是目前为止我用过的最好的调试工具。

✅ This is the best debugging tool I've ever used.

用户：This is the best debug tool I used until now.
问题：debug tool → debugging tool；I used until now → I've ever used（惯用表达）

> 语法：最高级 + the + 现在完成时 + ever，表达"迄今为止最…"的生命经历。

---

## 7. whether 名词从句 + 被动语态

🀄 我不确定这个 PR 今天能不能合进去，因为 CI 还在跑。

✅ I'm not sure whether this PR can be merged today because CI is still running.

用户：I'm not sure wether this PR can merged today bucause CI is still running.
问题：wether → whether；bucause → because；can merged → can be merged（被动缺 be）

> 语法：whether 引导宾语从句，陈述语序；被动语态 must be + done。

---

## 8. 定语从句 who + take over

🀄 那个写了核心模块的工程师昨天离职了，我们需要找人接手他的工作。

✅ The engineer who wrote the core module resigned yesterday. We need to find someone to take over his work.

用户：That engineer who write the core module resigned yesterday, we need find someone to recieve his job.
问题：write → wrote（时态）；need find → need to find；recieve → receive（拼写）；receive his job → take over his work（搭配）

> 语法：定语从句 who 指人做主语，时态需与主句一致（昨天 → 过去时）。

---

## 9. once 时间从句"主将从现"

🀄 一旦 QA 通过了这个版本，我们就立刻部署到生产环境。

✅ Once QA approves this version, we'll deploy it to production.

用户：Once the version passed by QA, we will depoly it to the production environment
问题：passed by QA → QA approves（时态+语态+搭配三重错）；depoly → deploy；production environment → production（简洁）

> 语法：时间状语从句"主将从现"，once 从句中用一般现在时代替将来。

---

## 10. 非谓语 to do 目的状语

🀄 为了赶在周五之前发布，我们跳过了好几轮代码审查。

✅ To ship before Friday, we skipped several rounds of code reviews.

> 语法：to do 不定式作目的状语（"为了…"），非谓语第29章核心。
