## Chunks Extracted

- **keep sb. updated**: 随时告知某人最新进展
- **additional context**: 补充背景信息
- **take sth. down from**: 从某处撤除/下架
- **what I'm trying to confirm is whether...**: 我想确认的是是否…
- **when you hear from them**: 当你收到他们的消息时

## Grammar Notes

- `keep + 宾语 + updated/informed`：使某人持续了解进展
- **What-clause 主语结构**：What I'm trying to confirm is whether... （比 "I want to confirm if..." 更正式）
- 口语化倒装：`if...is what I'm trying to confirm` → 正式写法应为 `What I'm trying to confirm is whether...`
