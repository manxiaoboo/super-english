## Chinese Intention

我想确认的是是否…（正式且礼貌的确认表达）

## Natural Chunk

what I'm trying to confirm is whether

## Avoid Saying



## Sentence Patterns

- What I'm trying to confirm is whether we installed the affected version.
- What I need to confirm is whether the patch was applied before the breach.
- What I'm trying to figure out is whether this impacts production.

## Real-Life Examples

- What I'm trying to confirm is whether we updated to the compromised version before it was taken down.
- What I need to confirm is whether our CI pipeline pulled the bad package.

## Usage Notes

What-clause 主语结构。比 'I want to confirm if...' 更正式地道。核心结构：What I'm trying to X is whether/how/why...
