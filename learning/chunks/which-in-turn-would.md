## Chinese Intention

进而会……（链式因果连接）

## Natural Chunk

which, in turn, would

## Avoid Saying



## Sentence Patterns

- ..., which, in turn, would lower profitability.
- ..., which, in turn, would increase costs significantly.

## Real-Life Examples

- Higher raw material prices, which, in turn, would reduce our profit margins.

## Usage Notes

in turn inserted as adverb phrase between which and verb. Signals chain reaction: A causes B, which in turn causes C.
