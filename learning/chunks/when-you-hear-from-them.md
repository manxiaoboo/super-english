## Sentence Patterns

- Let me know when you hear from them.

- Reach out when you hear from the vendor.

## Real-Life Examples

- Please keep me updated about the CCW team when you hear from them.

## Usage Notes

hear from sb. = 收到某人的联系（消息/邮件/电话）。区分 hear from sb. vs hear about sth.。
