## Meaning

用间接问句结构表达做某事的难易程度，整体作名词从句

## Usage Context

直接问句：How easy is it for suppliers to raise prices?
间接从句（名词从句，陈述语序）：Here you assess how easy it is for your supplier to raise its prices.

关键：疑问词 how + 形容词 + it is + for sb + to do，内部不倒装。
常见触发词：assess, examine, look at, study, determine, understand, see, find out

对比：
✓ how easy it is to enter the market
✗ how easy is it to enter the market (倒装只用于直接问句)

## Anchor Sentence

Here you assess how easy it is for your supplier to raise its prices.
